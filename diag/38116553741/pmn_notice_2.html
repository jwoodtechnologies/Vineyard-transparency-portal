Description/Agenda
NOTICE OF A REGULAR CITY COUNCIL MEETING October 13, 2026, at 6:00 PM PUBLIC NOTICE is hereby given that the Vineyard City Council will hold a regularly scheduled City Council meeting on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, at 6:00 PM, in the City Council Chambers at City Hall, 125 South Main Street, Vineyard, UT. This meeting can also be viewed on our live stream page. AGENDA 1. CALL TO ORDER 2. INVOCATION AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE (BY INVITATION) 3. PUBLIC COMMENTS (3 MINUTES PER PERSON) 'Public Comments' is defined as time set aside for citizens to express their views. Public comments can be submitted ahead of time to robinr@vineyardutah.gov. 4. WORK SESSION 4.1 Discuss Short-Term Rentals (Ed Kennedy - 10 Minutes) 5. CONSENT ITEMS 5.1. Approval of the September 15, 2026, City Council Meeting Minutes 6. APPOINTMENTS AND REMOVALS 6.1. Appoint ARCH Commission Member (Brian Vawdreoy - 5 Minutes) 7. BUSINESS ITEMS 7.1. Approve RES 2026-40 - Awarding the Parking Enforcement Bid to EZ Park (5 Minutes) 7.2. Approve RES 2026-41 Establishing the Vineyard Economic Development Partnership (VEDP) (5 Minutes) 7.3. Approve ORD 2026-12 Renaming the Active Transportation Commission (ATC) as the Pedestrian Safety Commission (PSC) (5 Minutes) 7.4. Membership in the North Utah County Aquifer Council (NUCAC) (5 Minutes) Consideration and approval of a resolution authorizing Vineyard City's membership in the North Utah County Aquifer Council (NUCAC), at an initial cost of $12,000 and approximately $6,000 annually thereafter. 7.5. Accept Resolution 2026-36: Opposing The Geneva Retention Pond Exchange And Requesting A Public Management Plan (5 Minutes) 8. ADJOURNMENT
Notice of Special Accommodations (ADA)
SPECIAL ACCOMMODATION DURING PUBLIC MEETINGS The Public is invited to participate in all City Council meetings. In compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, individuals needing special accommodations during this meeting should notify the Vineyard Recorder, at least 24 hours prior to the meeting by calling (801) 226-1929.
Notice of Electronic or Telephone Participation
POSSIBLE ELECTRONIC OR TELEPHONIC PARTICIPATION This meeting may be held in a way that will allow a councilmember to participate electronically. EXECUTIVE SESSION The City Council may vote to go into Executive Session pursuant to Utah State Code 52-4-204

Definitions

  • Government Type: A categorization of government entities, including state, county, municipality, special districts, and schools, among others. The designation of special district includes all local districts and special service districts. There are many different designations, and the term special district is only used as a common term.
  • Entity: All state agencies, political subdivisions (cities, counties, special districts), the legislature, judiciary, and public education or state-funded higher education institutions.
  • Public Body: A board, commission, or committee that posts notices or holds meetings, according to the Open and Public Meetings Act, Utah Code 52-4-103(7)
